# Story introduction cards in mobile fullscreen and perfect-clear gradients

Story introductions now rearrange their cards when the number of rows or columns changes. Previously, a four-card introduction opened in portrait kept its two-column coordinates after switching to landscape fullscreen. The short single-row table clamped both previous rows near the same vertical center, leaving two pairs of cards almost completely overlapping. Replaying the former layout function at 390 × 844 → 844 × 390 reproduces more than 90% overlap within both pairs.

Introduction cards now use absolute positions within the scrolling card table. CSS converts the shared gesture handlers' viewport coordinates to that table's local coordinates, accounting for its border. Cards follow panel scrolling without fixed-position clipping or doubled offsets. Card sizing also accounts for their rotated extents. Positions manually adjusted by the player are retained while the row/column arrangement stays the same; a changed arrangement lays out all cards again so every card is reachable.

Fullscreen entry/exit, visual viewport changes and a table `ResizeObserver` refresh the layout. Observer and visual viewport notifications are coalesced into an animation frame. Closing an introduction disconnects its observer, removes its listeners and cancels its pending frame; stale notifications cannot reopen it.

Perfect-clear stars now have a static light–dark–light gradient in both chapter selection and the match result. Their original faction palette remains the base: chapter six is gold, and chapter eight keeps pink Kasen, dark gold Matara and deep purple Yukari as independent star colors. Stars accumulated across separate victories keep their ordinary presentation. Saved progress and the single-winning-match perfect-clear condition are unchanged. Browsers without text clipping or color mixing retain solid stars, and forced-color mode uses system text colors.

Validation: **786 checks in 14 related suites**, including 115 new checks. The new suite exercises the real introduction layout and inspection handlers for chapters 2–12 at eight viewport sizes, portrait/fullscreen transitions without a window resize event, both edge cards, scrolling, rotated bounds, viewport/observer updates, cleanup, star persistence and result rendering. Existing story progress, Sage colors, gold stars, introductions, preludes, card physics, settings, objective paper and language checks also pass. The settings fixture now models multiple independent resize observers instead of replacing one observer callback with the latest callback.

These are controlled DOM, geometry and CSS checks; physical-phone pixels and performance have not been measured. Core game rules, AI policies and turn flow are byte-identical to the prior commit. Rules version remains `2026-10-18-story-discard-objectives-v33`.
